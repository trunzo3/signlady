Meet Maria

From $2 and a Dream to The Sign Lady

In 1998, I started Absolute Promotions with just $2 in my bank account, two children to raise, and a determination to succeed. I built my business through hard work, creativity, and relationships.

More than 30 years later, I still love helping businesses stand out and get noticed. My greatest success isn't just the business I've built. It's the people I've helped along the way.
